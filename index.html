Care and formation

Services at the Centre

Wherever you are on your journey, there is a place to be heard, encouraged and equipped.

GENERAL CONSULTATION

A safe space for individuals from diverse religious backgrounds to consult on aspects of faith and life issues.

PRAYER LINE

Connect with trained ministers who are ready to listen, offer comfort, and pray for and with you regarding your specific needs. Help is always just a call away.

CHRISTIAN TRAINING PROGRAMS

Courses and programs to edify and equip you on your faith journey. We walk with you to deepen your understanding of scripture and core doctrines while acquiring practical skills for spiritual growth.

MINISTRY TRAINING PROGRAMS & MANAGEMENT

Designed for ministers called to serve, build, and steward with excellence. This includes:

  • Ministry Consultation
  • Ministerial Institute, an education platform
  • Ministry Partnership, Collaboration and Fundraising to form alliances and develop sustainable fundraising strategies

SANCTUARY RETREAT & CONSECRATION CAMP

Designed for personal edification and spiritual growth. Sanctuary Retreat offers space to step away from the noise of the world and tune in to the Father. Consecration Camp is a three-day program at the end of each month for repentance, purification, and sanctification.

SUBSIDIZED SUBSCRIPTION PACKAGES

Services throughout the Centre designed to nurture you spiritually, mentally, and physically for a balanced, faith-centred life. Free and paid options are available.

Programs

Regular moments of prayer, worship, reflection, and community, alongside focused outreach programs.

At Manus Dei United

Fresh Start Devotion

From the 1st to the 8th of each month, we engage in prayer, fasting, and reflection, blocking out distractions, seeking clarity, and preparing spiritually for the month ahead.

RAIN

Every Sunday, we gather for worship, intercession, and prophetic ministration, creating space to encounter God's presence and receive divine insight.

Friday Reflections

Every Friday, we come together to pause, reflect, recalibrate, and realign to God's direction.

Tongues Festival

Every 14th to 16th of each month, we immerse ourselves in the Spirit for 48 hours. At every quarter of the day, we pray in the Spirit, stir spiritual gifts, and receive strength and renewal.

At Manus Dei Ministry

RAIN Open-Air Outreach

A week-long evangelistic program reaching underserved and vulnerable communities with the love and message of Jesus Christ. It culminates in a Holy Ghost Prayer and Revival Meeting marked by worship, healing, and the prophetic.

Exoneration Conference

A Spirit-empowered conference rooted in Romans 8:1, declaring liberty over guilt, shame, and spiritual bondage through worship, prophetic teaching, and Spirit-led intercession.

Reflections

A contemplative program offering space for spiritual clarity and renewal through worship, teaching, and Spirit-led dialogue.

Balm of Gilead Crusade

Inspired by Jeremiah 8:22 and Isaiah 61:1, an outreach where the wounded are healed, the broken restored, and captives set free by the power of Jesus Christ.
